# The signal cone audit — the light cone carries no violation (Nadel Ⅶ)

## Abstract

One Blatt, one verdict: the full run of the signal cone audit finds no fam arrow that violates the light cone. We ask whether any measured information flow carries a transit time τ shorter than the light transit time d/c across the ICRS distance of its two mouths. The machine measures the betrayal of c and finds none: every transfer-entropy arrow lies within its cone. The silence is the measurement (0 honored).


## The question

Does any measured information flow violate the d/c law? A TE arrow whose
transit time τ is shorter than the light transit time d/c across the
ICRS distance of its two mouths would be the break of the cone — the
wormhole signal of Nadel Ⅶ. The machine measures the betrayal of c and
explains nothing.

## The machine and the run

`signalkegel_audit_probe` — 10 channels (Nadel-Ⅲ-DAG: F10.7, XRSA, XRSB,
Lya1216, Bz, density, Vwind at the solar anchor; Wind/WAVES RAD1/RAD2/TNR at
the L1 anchor), 90 pairs, n ≈ 3824 daily cells, fam round (10

× 8 lags), PE gate (2⁴ ring across 256-cell segments), the cone check per
fam pair over `signal_reach` (em → c, advective → the measured
wind speed, diffusion → √(2·D·τ)), the negative-lag scan, the
funnel crosscheck with Nadel Ⅵ. Anchors measured: d(sun↔wind) = 1,462e11 m
= 0,9771 AU — the only non-degenerate anchor distance.

The run computed detached (release, ~6,5 h) on the new meter-exact
ephemeris bins (CDN since 2026-08-23T13:37; the previous run on the
truncated bins was named and restarted). Log:
`data/kegel_audit_voll.log`.

## The finding

- **No fam pair carries an arrow** — the audit table stays empty;
  the cone is tested by no arrow. A violation would have carried a
  fam arrow with τ < d/c; there is none.
- **Reverse-direction rows:** 1 fam arrow in the matrix — the
  direction picture, no τ<0 finding, named.
- **The only PE caveat:** Vwind → density (lag 0 d) carries a
  PE jump of the driver — the arrow carries the caveat, no cone check.
- **Funnel crosscheck (Nadel Ⅵ):** the jerk arrows of the Sonden-Front
  are none today — Paths 1 and 2 stay closed (pre-registration
  sealed); the shared table stays empty.
- **Minimum τ against the same cone:** em 492,0 s, advective 4,3 d,
  diffusion 2,18e23 s — no law carries both paths; that remains the
  architecture of the touchstone, not a finding.

## The statement

The d/c law holds. The light cone carries no violation — 90 pairs,
10 channels, n ≈ 3824 daily-cell common window (~10 years; the F10.7
record alone spans 79 years, the common window does not), no exception.
The silence is the measurement:
0 honored, and the search remains — the register carries the next round
of the audit when new channels join.

## Limitations

- **Surrogate-machine generation (conservative footnote).** The verdict is
  fam-governed: the cone is tested by no arrow, but no family-bound value
  is printed here (fam-round-machine: pre-fix — verdict fam-governed,
  value unprinted). The bound was generated by the pre-fix surrogate RNG.
  The pre-fix band lies higher than the post-fix band; a verdict that
  holds against the higher pre-fix bound holds post-fix a fortiori. The
  measured silence is thus the conservative statement.
