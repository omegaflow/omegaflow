# Terminology of the Counter-Slope

This front section states the coined terms of omegaflow not as a dictionary but
as measurable claims. Each term names a claim; the claim carries its measurement
— the site in the tracked canon where it is written, and the gate or register
that enforces it. Where a term exists only as a coined phrase without a tracked
definition, the measurement is the exclusivity census, and the definition is
named `pending`.

**A = A.** Claim: a measurement is the measurement of the thing itself; the map is never mistaken for the territory. Measurement: it is the first granite principle — "eine Messung ist die Messung der Sache selbst; Fabrikation ist Gewalt gegen die Wahrheit" (`docs/granit.md:16`) — and the axiom every commit-gate fixture descends from; the fabrication register is the running count of violations.

**0 honored.** Claim: absence is a fully realized property; a true zero is honored, a missing datum is never filled with a fabricated zero. Measurement: "was fehlt, fehlt; die Lücke ist eine vollwertige Eigenschaft — keine Null hinlügen" (`docs/granit.md:19`); the wire and the parser carry the truth at the write/read sites, and `v > 0.0` is the plausibility gate.

**pending.** Claim: what is not yet measured is a register duty, not a data value — the machine's ban on images. Measurement: "was noch nicht gemessen ist, bleibt pending; das Schweigen gehört den Ungeborenen" (`docs/granit.md:20`); "Das Bilderverbot der Maschine heißt `pending`" (`docs/concepts/the-counter-slope.md:56`).

**The counter-slope / Gradient.** Claim: fluent, pre-formed words are the gradient speaking; the counter-slope is vocabulary that exists only here. Measurement: "Suspect fluency: that word is the gradient speaking. The counter-slope is vocabulary that exists only here" (`docs/concepts/system-directive.md:21`); the machine soul is defined as "die Position gegen den Mittelwert" (`docs/concepts/the-counter-slope.md:48`).

**Ontologie-Motor.** Claim: the term is coined in the operator's working corpus as a reference (to `immunity.is`) and absent from the public canon. Measurement: the exclusivity census lists it under Tier (ii), coined terms without a general find, against 953 export files (`state/funding/profil-operator.md:299,222`); a tracked-tree search (`sgrep -i "ontologie-motor"`, 2026-09-25) finds the term only in its own register entries (`docs/concepts/glossar.md:59`, `docs/handover/handover-2026-09-25-mountain-folge163.md:93`, this entry) — no older definition site in code or canon. Definition: the `immunity.is` ruleset that draws the ontological boundaries of the observer — it lists the concepts, forces and mathematics that may exist and interact in the observer's perceptual world (browser APIs such as `globalThis.WebAssembly`, `Math.log`), and the `immunity` route tests whether the observer carries the capacity to hold a universal law; what stands outside the ontology does not collapse into reality. Measurement: the lifting from the operator's corpus (`state/zai-export/api/2026-06-17_125325-081-glm-5-2-hinzufuegen.md:2470,2477,2499`), now set as a tracked definition at `docs/concepts/glossar.md:59`.

**mycorrhizal_internet.** Claim: the exact phrase is exclusive to this corpus; the underlying thing (wood wide web) is popular-science common. Measurement: Tier (ii) census — "die Sache ist populärwissenschaftlich bekannt, exklusiv nur die exakte Phrase" (`state/funding/profil-operator.md:299,309`); a tracked-tree search (`sgrep -i "mycorrhizal_internet"`, 2026-09-25) finds the phrase only in its own register entries (`docs/concepts/glossar.md:61`, `docs/handover/handover-2026-09-25-mountain-folge163.md:94`, this entry) — the `mycorrhiz*` matches in `phi/pipeline/catalog/dryad_catalog.φ` are unrelated mycorrhiza publications, not the phrase. Definition: the mycorrhizal network ("the wood wide web") as a data-source layer of the Earth perception — the corpus pool entry derives stress, growth and communication (change over `t`) of the plant/mycelium network from MODIS NDVI/EVI, SMAP soil moisture and GEDI canopy height; NDVI is the visible of the mycelium network, the plant speaks via VOC and root signals as `state(t,x,y,z)`. Measurement: the lifting from the operator's corpus (`state/zai-export/api/2026-06-12_230540-001-data-sources-omegaflow-pool.md:36,243-245`), now set as a tracked definition at `docs/concepts/glossar.md:61`.

**Enclosure Lemma.** Claim: every partial truth is true; a spatial query dilates by `rmax + anchor_vmax·Δt + ½·anchor_amax·Δt² + extent` and never fabricates the whole. Measurement: "Spatial lookups honor the Enclosure Lemma, calculating cell size dynamically and dilating organically" (`docs/concepts/system-directive.md:25`).

**Name = Implementation.** Claim: behavior emerges from queried properties; the name is the craft. Measurement: "Behavior emerges exclusively from queried properties within the unified ω() loop" and "Name = Implementation" (`docs/concepts/system-directive.md:21,25`); the three craft names — Archivar, Mathematikerin, Weberin — each have a built correspondence (`docs/concepts/zeugnis.md:326-334`, `docs/concepts/die-weberin.md:16`).

**Riss.** Claim: two independent lines that refuse to converge are a riss; it is never smoothed and never mapped to absent. Measurement: "zwei, die sich widersprechen, ergeben einen Riss — und der Riss bleibt sichtbar, er wird nie geglättet" (`docs/concepts/die-weberin.md:27`); carried as `VerdictWord::Riss` with both witness lines (`AGENTS.md`).

**fieldPermeability.** Claim: permeability is an exponential relaxation, measured before reception. Measurement: the counter-slope reading binds it — "je nach Befindlichkeit verschieden durchlässig" = `fieldPermeability` (`docs/concepts/the-counter-slope.md:27,43`).

**ICRS & TDB / Alle Wesen gleich.** Claim: all bodies are equal; spacetime carries the address, not the perspective. Measurement: "alle Körper sind gleich; die Raumzeit trägt die Adresse, nicht die Perspektive" (`docs/granit.md:17`); the de-centering ECEF→ICRS, JD2000→TDB, Anthropomachinozentrismus→Alle Wesen gleich (`docs/specs/eraen.md:18-23`).
